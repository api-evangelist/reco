# Reco

> Reco is the AI Agent Security platform that discovers, secures, and monitors every AI agent, non-human identity, and SaaS application across 225+ integrations. The platform maps every agent in your environment to its owner, permissions, and risk score, and addresses AI sprawl, agent sprawl, and configuration sprawl in real time. Reco brings agent security, identity governance, and threat detection together in one platform, with 1,000+ pre-built detection controls, no-code support for new apps in 3-5 days, and auto-remediation through your existing stack.

Reco secures the way enterprises actually use AI today. The platform delivers four outcomes: complete agent inventory across SaaS, IDP, API, network, and browser sources; identity and access governance for both human and non-human identities; data exposure management; and threat detection and response. Three proprietary technologies power the platform: Unified Discovery™ finds every agent and app from every source, App Factory™ adds new apps and agent platforms in 3-5 days with no code, and the Knowledge Graph turns every identity, permission, connection, and event into a living map of risk. Reco has raised $85M total after Series B and is trusted by Fortune 500 enterprises and high-growth companies globally.

## The Three Sprawls Reco Solves

- **AI Sprawl** – Embedded AI in SaaS apps, GenAI tools, and copilots expanding faster than security teams can track.
- **Agent Sprawl** – AI agents deployed across the environment faster than any security team can review or remediate.
- **Configuration Sprawl** – AI features introducing new permissions and policies that drift from their original state.
